Alongside the contribution of CLPR to LF Decentralized Trust, Hashgraph opened a call for participants in its Cross-Ledger Early Adopter Program.

## Three use cases

The program is built around three use cases:

- cross-border payments and in-flight FX
- cross-ledger settlement
- collateral mobility

## How it runs

It runs as a three-phase virtual program. Participants get access to a Hashgraph-hosted testnet, where they move test assets through a CLPR-enabled application layer, and technical participants work with an assigned integration expert.

## Who can take part

Financial institutions, fintechs, asset issuers, liquidity providers and corporations can take part. The program aims to select participants from several countries, so that cross-border regulatory and compliance questions are part of the work from the start.

The program is run by Hashgraph, not by the CLPR lab, and organizations register their interest on [Hashgraph’s site](https://hashgraph.com/clpr-early-adopter-program/). The protocol itself is developed in the open in the [LFDT-CLPR repositories](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR), where anyone can contribute; the [Issue Explorer](/issues/) lists the open issues.
