On 24 September 2026, Hedera contributed CLPR (“clipper”) to LF Decentralized Trust as a new lab. CLPR was developed by the Hashgraph team. It is a cross-ledger messaging protocol: it uses state proofs so that independent ledger networks can verify each other’s state, without a bridge or an intermediary validator set in the middle.

## An openly developed codebase

As an LFDT lab, CLPR is developed in the open. The [specification](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR/clpr-spec), the [Solidity contracts](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR/clpr-smart-contracts) for EVM ledgers, the [Java endpoint](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR/clpr-endpoint) and the lab’s [governance](/governance/) are in the [LFDT-CLPR organization](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR) on GitHub. Labs are where early work builds a community and invites contributions before it can become a top-level LFDT project, and CLPR is early: the specification is still changing and some economic parameters are open.

## Get involved

To get started, read the [specification](https://github.com/LFDT-CLPR/clpr-spec), pick an open issue in the [Issue Explorer](/issues/), or join the [weekly call](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/lf-decentralized-trust-labs?view=week). [The plumbing of CLPR](/plumbing/) explains the protocol from the ground up.
